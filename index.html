FRAGBAZ

An arena FPS and engine in development.
Combat, teamwork, and maps you can make your own.

Early build.
Real testing.

This pre-alpha is suitable only for testers. Expect unfinished systems, bugs, and breaking changes.

The current build does not support 1,024 players in a single arena. Supporting that population at 60 Hz remains a development target.

Almost all current assets are temporary and will be replaced with original art, models, animations, and audio.
